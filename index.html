AI story 1 · RAG agent

The questions only experts could answer

The problem

Onboarding a new US health system meant digging through scattered product, technical, integration, deployment and process documents. New team members depended on experienced colleagues, the same questions came up again and again, and onboarding slowed down.

Why RAG

The answers were specific to the company, current and contextual, which a general LLM doesn't know. Retrieval keeps every answer grounded in approved internal sources, traceable back to them, and up to date without retraining.

What I built

An agent that takes a plain-language question, retrieves the right internal knowledge and returns a grounded answer with its sources. I framed the opportunity, defined the workflow and success criteria, structured how knowledge is retrieved, wrote the prompts and checked whether the answers held up.

10 → 6 months

Time to onboard a new US health system, about 40% faster.

How it workedShow less
  • Less dependency on experienced team members for repeat questions.
  • Faster search across fragmented documentation.
  • Reusable across other onboarding and deployment workflows.

AI story 2 · LLM-powered clinical tool

An AI that clinicians would actually trust

The problem

In a clinical setting, an LLM is only useful if its output is reliable, easy to check and fits how clinicians already work. A capable model was not enough. The workflow around it had to make the output trustworthy.

The approach

Define what "good" means before shipping: what the model should do, how to tell when its output is right, and where the clinician stays in control. I designed the prompts, the evaluation criteria and the clinician interaction, with a human in the loop, and tested assumptions against real cases.

40% → 70%

Follow-up rate after the LLM-powered workflow went live.

How it workedShow less
  • Consistent: structured prompts made outputs more predictable.
  • Measurable: clear eval criteria defined what "good" means.
  • Workflow-fit: built into how clinicians already work, with them reviewing before acting.

AI story 3 · Automation

Every demo, built by hand

The problem

Every customer conversation needed a demo and deck tailored to that health system's use case. Building them by hand was slow and repetitive, and quality varied depending on who made them.

The insight

Most of every deck was the same: product facts, the value story, the demo flow. Only the customer context changed, so AI could combine the two on demand.

What I built

An AI workflow that turns customer context and product knowledge into a tailored demo script and pitch deck, with templates and a quick human review so it stays accurate and on-brand.

↓ Product bandwidth

The product team no longer builds each new product demo and deck by hand.

How it workedShow less
  • Consistent story and accurate messaging, whoever presents.
  • Tailored at scale: every prospect gets material built for their use case.
  • Time back for the customer conversation instead of formatting.

Product story · Care.Fi

The six-hour wait to go home

The problem

For insured patients, discharge waits on paperwork. The hospital collects the final bill, discharge summary, ID and policy documents, sends them to the insurer or TPA, and waits for approval. Done by hand across email and portals, with documents often missing, this took around six hours. Patients waited and beds stayed blocked.

The insight

Most of the delay came from handoffs and incomplete files, not from the insurer's decision. If the file was complete the first time and submitted and tracked digitally, most of the waiting would go away.

What we built

A digital case file per admission, completeness checks for each insurer, pre-filled pre-authorisation forms, one submission flow to insurers and TPAs, and live status tracking with alerts for the billing desk.

6 hrs → 30 min

Discharge turnaround for cashless patients, through faster document submission and pre-authorisation.

How it workedShow less
Under the hood
  • Digital case file. One record per admission, with the bill, discharge summary, ID and policy documents uploaded or scanned in.
  • Completeness checks. Per-insurer checklists and field validation before submission, so gaps are caught at the hospital.
  • Pre-filled forms. Pre-authorisation and discharge forms generated from hospital and policy data in each insurer's format.
  • Submission and tracking. One submission flow to insurer and TPA channels, with a case timeline, SLA timers and alerts when queries or approvals arrive.
  • Ops dashboard. Turnaround per case, stuck cases, and query reasons by insurer.
